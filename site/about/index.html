About

Computer engineer, Flutter and Supabase.

I build production mobile and web systems, and I like the problems that live at the boundary between a database schema and the screen on top of it.

Background

I graduated in Computer Engineering from the Faculty of Engineering at Shoubra, Benha University, in 2021. My graduation project was GrouBuy, a group-buying e-commerce platform that let buyers aggregate demand to unlock volume pricing.

Since February 2025 I have worked as a software engineer building and maintaining a production ERP for HR and employee management. I own modules end to end — requirements and fit-gap analysis, workflow modelling, schema design, implementation, and delivery. Working as the sole developer on a system that people depend on daily has shaped how I work: I would rather spend an afternoon modelling a workflow with the people who use it than a week rewriting it after the fact.

I am currently architecting a retail property management platform that extends that ERP, adding internal Finance, Legal and RDD modules alongside a separate tenant-facing Flutter application running on the same Supabase project.

How I work

  • Constraints belong in the database. Access rules live in Row Level Security policies, not in the client. If the UI is the only thing standing between a user and someone else's data, that is a bug waiting to happen.
  • Model the workflow before building it. Diagramming state transitions with stakeholders catches disagreements while they still cost nothing.
  • Keep the seams clean. Repositories are abstract interfaces with swappable implementations. It pays for itself in testing, and occasionally in ways you did not plan for.
